## Calcium Imaging Microscopy Application

- Core Technologies: Arduino, FPGA, SCARA robotic arm, and video motion tracking algorithms
- Project Description: A collaborator-developed [TINIscope headset](https://academic.oup.com/nsr/article/doi/10.1093/nsr/nwad294/7438875) was used to observe brain activity while mice interacted freely. To support wider movement, the cable connected to the head-mounted device was attached to a SCARA robotic arm that followed the animal. Behavioral videos and calcium imaging videos were recorded together for further analysis.
- Key Responsibilities:
  - Deployed SLEAP-based motion tracking for real-time video analysis
  - Used Arduino to control the movement of the SCARA robotic arm according to the mouse position
